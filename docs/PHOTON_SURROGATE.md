# Reduced photoreceptor pilot (2026-09-23)

The full published MaleCNS connectome is unchanged. This work tests only a cheaper sensory boundary in place of the current 30,000-microvillus-per-receptor VisTrans execution. The original source has **not** passed the visual biological gate; matching it is an engineering comparison, not proof of in-vivo fidelity. No reduced candidate is in the production loop or authorized as the final fly.

## Monte Carlo microvillus sample

`tools/build_photon_sampled.py` builds an isolated CUDA diagnostic that retains the published reaction law per simulated microvillus. The photon hazard uses the physical count of 30,000 and the computed current is scaled by 30,000 / sampled count. At 30,000 the 16-receptor, 100-step checkpoint payload and observation matched the original byte for byte. This is a Monte Carlo approximation at smaller counts, with inflated sampling noise.

For 6091 receptors and 100 steps, Molab measured 0.0739 s at 256 microvilli (89× versus the previously measured 6.5717 s), 0.2538 s at 1024 (26×), and 0.9248 s at 4096 (7.1×). These are isolated sensory times at 50,000 photons/s and one seed. The response protocol used 100 ms gray, 20 ms light or dark, 200 ms gray, and three independent seeds. The population-mean response RMSE as a fraction of the original response peak was 15.6–21.9% for light at 256, 7.1–11.0% at 1024, and 3.3–3.9% at 4096; the dark errors were 6.2–9.5%, 2.5–4.8%, and 1.4–1.8%, respectively.

Critically, the original model's receptor-level pairwise seed variation was about 1.15–1.21 mV, while the sampled 4096 model differed from its paired original by 2.27–2.52 mV, 1024 by 4.42–4.94 mV, and 256 by 8.77–10.10 mV. Even the 4096 approximation changes the stochastic signal materially. It is **not adopted**. ## Learned deterministic pilot

`tools/collect_photon_surrogate_teacher.py` generated full 30,000-microvillus teacher traces for 64 receptors under independent piecewise light levels (0, 1k, 10k, 50k, 100k photons/s, 5–150 ms holds). Training used a four-second stream, and holdout used a different two-second stream and photon RNG seed. Both had a 500 ms gray prelude. The data and source are archived at private HF revision `d39442d9ba8fbe777ab9c2d7ad7304919d3f4eae`, manifest `manifests/627c43596d31dd8ffdb5595f04b95942cb9fc02842574ff5f2a66b0293e99bb9.json`.

`tools/train_photon_surrogate_pilot_v2.py` trains a 24-state GRU in PyTorch to predict receptor voltage from photon rate history at 1 ms resolution. After 200 epochs its held-out voltage RMSE was 1.620 mV; its p95 absolute error was 2.934 mV. The weights, predictions, source, and report are archived at private HF revision `fce98a1fb2280e5577b1e38b437c23a6faca71fe`, manifest `manifests/959d8db130be3623e229e51f60cd8ab0cd9239d7f0194793c1bd0fdc5ae84e2d.json`. This model is deterministic and cannot reproduce intrinsic receptor noise.

An out-of-distribution test used a fresh seed, 500 ms gray, 20 ms flash, and 500 ms gray. The model's per-receptor whole-episode RMSE was 1.28 mV for light and 1.37 mV for dark. The sign-aware light peak was 7.49 mV versus 8.70 mV in the source, and its opposite phase was 0.537 versus 0.944 mV. Dark peak was −19.99 versus −19.67 mV, with opposite phase 2.249 versus 2.405 mV. Thus the light peak is about 14% low and its opposite phase about 43% low. The pilot **fails** a morphology-preservation requirement even though its aggregate RMSE is small. Flash traces and online timing are archived at private HF revision `5cb9c98a40d7b8b3394acaa9fe903e5bcf40593f`, manifest `manifests/3835783f007997f7e5332f4c12e294313df65538bd90ffb70f7c6f4d3118db5c.json`; sign-aware phase analysis is in the derived-analysis manifest above.

Online PyTorch GRU execution for 6091 receptors took 0.112 s for 1000 sequential 1 ms updates, versus about 657 s for a simulated second of the isolated original photoreceptor model under its 0.1 ms update. This ~5900× comparison shows the possible scale of a sensory surrogate, **not** end-to-end fly speed. It changes the sensory update resolution, omits stochastic noise and neural feedback, and excludes CNS, body, environment and recording. No Rust/CUDA production port exists.

The next gate is an improved causal sensory model trained and tested across graded flashes, continuous naturalistic changes, endogenous feedback, multiple intensity regimes, and independent seeds. Evaluate mean waveform, peaks and opposite phases, receptor-to-receptor variance, temporal correlations and downstream CNS responses; recheck against independent biological physiology. Only after those gates may a reduced retina be used for training, with full VisTrans replay retained as a separate final validation. The full connectome remains mandatory throughout.

Subsequent numerical diagnostics in `PHOTON_NUMERICS.md` found that the teacher used here holds light current fixed for each 0.1 ms receptor tick, producing high-flux voltage overshoot. The learned pilot reproduces that **unvalidated teacher**. If the within-substep current-coupling candidate passes its independent gates, the teacher dataset and surrogate must be regenerated and reevaluated; the present accuracy and speed results do not transfer automatically.
